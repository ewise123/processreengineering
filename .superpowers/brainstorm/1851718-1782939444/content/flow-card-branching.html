Stepper — gateways, branches & rework

A flow is really a small graph. Below: a gateway with a Yes/No split + a rework loop-back, and a 3-way amount gateway. Two ways to render the branches — click the one you prefer. (Gateway = amber diamond; rework = "↩ back to" badge; proposed step = dashed violet NEW.)

A

Indented branches

Each gateway's paths become indented sub-flows under it, headed by their condition. Most faithful; a rework path ends with a "↩ back to" badge instead of drawing a line upward.
